# The Gates — Heaven & Hell (2026-09-12): the arc SHIPPED

THE GATES ARC was designed on 2026-09-12 and **built whole the same day,
across five sessions**. This file was its spec companion, written in
worldsim.md's shape: a section was CUT when the session that built it
landed. Every section has now landed, so what is left is this note and a
map of where the content went.

Unlike `worldsim.md`, which kept its undesigned residue after its last
rung, this file keeps none: nothing in it went unbuilt. The three
questions the arc deliberately did not answer are roadmap items, not spec
residue, and they live in `plan.md`.

## The five sessions

1. **The four sites on the map** (designlog (A)) — the roll, the rings,
   the two ruins as dungeons, `delve`, the foe skins. This is the one that
   made the arc playable.
2. **The two sentinels and the ruin jobs** (designlog (B)) — Zohariel and
   Saar, the two bars Tom set, the eight board jobs into the ruins.
3. **The Nephilim** (designlog (C)) — half of all player characters are
   half-blood, and the setting walks in with the party.
4. **The two city states** (designlog (D)) — the world is eleven lands.
5. **The packets and the human side** (designlog (E)) — what the two
   colonies DO, and what the nine make of them.

## The directives this arc stood on

Kept here because they were recorded as standing and outlive the build:

- **The pact is a gimmick and this arc ignored it.** The Hell contract and
  its enforcement stay in the game, nothing in this arc had to fit them,
  and rules.md's Hell Pact section now points at the add-on as the setting
  authority. Whether the pact is rewritten onto this setting or cut is a
  later sitting (plan.md).
- **The low-band-only principle was set aside for this arc**, because the
  start level is rolled and the content spans the ladder.
- **Four one-tile city states, semi-random, conquest later.** Nothing here
  built conquest, and the standing wars are still not rolled over either
  city state.
