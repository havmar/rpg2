## turn 2 (day 1)

> take the warband job

Varg shakes on it and points you north. The trail is an hour out of the village: churned grass, a dead horse with its haunches cut away, and the watch posted where the ground rises. Three men. They see you coming and do not run.

```

DEX 6  STR 5  STA 7  HP 13/13
takes spoils, leaves the fallen
2x War Chief -- longsword
DEX 7  STR 6  STA 8  HP 17/17
drilled +2,
takes spoils, leaves the fallen
```

[the full fight, round by round](https://github.com/havmar/rpg2/blob/claude/new-game-rhc7vd/ui/fight-short.txt)

Your ice takes the veteran twice and holds him frozen both times, and he never swings once. Gell's sweep catches him and a chief together and kills him outright. Then the second chief gets inside Gell's guard three rounds running: ribs, back, belly. Gell is still on his feet with his zweihander up. Your Power is gone.
