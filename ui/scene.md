## turn 3 (day 3)

> travel back to Ordubal and take the hellgate job

You come back down the salt road on the third morning. Myev the fixer keeps a stall of secondhand harness behind the market and has never been seen to sell any of it. She does not ask your name, and she has your instructions written out already: a mark drawn on a scrap of hide, and the words to say over it.

The place is an old threshing floor an hour east of the walls, where the basin's stone comes up through the dirt. "The ones on the far side won't know whose you are," Myev says. "Don't take it personally."

The floor is flat rock with a ring cut into it, half swallowed by salt grass. Somebody has been camped on it a long while: a fire pit, split bones, three lean-tos of hide and driftwood. What stands up at the edge of the ring is already hell's, and nobody has told it so.

It never lands a blow. Gwen takes the claws on the katana three exchanges running and opens it wider each time, and every round the wounds close again as fast as they open. Your fire is what settles it: two clean bolts through the same shoulder, faster than the knitting. It goes over the rocks toward the east with one arm hanging, and it is still going.
