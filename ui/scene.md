## turn 1 (day 1)

> please start a new game

You are Mindartis: an elf, twenty-two, years on the road with a masterwork katana on your hip and ice at your fingertips. The kit is good and the purse is not empty. Years ago you signed a contract with Hell -- wealth and power, obedience owed. The work has been coming ever since.

Feng has walked at your side for most of it. An orc, loud, dressed better than the job ever requires, a rapier drilled to a point. He brews potions when there is time for it.

You are in Bjorgheim, the dwarven capital, walled stone where the mountain roads meet. A hard wind comes down off the peaks and the market has its hoods up.

Fargrim, head runesmith, catches you outside the smithy with soot on his forearms and no interest in small talk. A crew of mages broke the seal on the old workings above the town, he says, and lit something in there that will not go out. It burns wet stone. It burns without air. He wants the mages dead and the fire smothered, and he wants it done before the burn reaches the tomb galleries. The deep galleries are graves. Graves are the holy ground here.

```
  job: Mages in the Mine -- L6
  giver: Fargrim, head runesmith
  1 site, 3 fights. foes: ice and
    fire mages
  pay: 238g. due day 4 (3 left)
```

In the market a border guardsman out of Gibili is telling anyone who slows down that an orc warlord has united the clans and his raiders are burning guard posts and mapping the roads. Feng folds his arms and lets the man talk.

```
  WORD OF THE WAR: the Iron Horde.
  Wozzek, chief overboss, calls for
    blades at Maketawa:
  [w1] L2 Orc Scouts.
```

By the tannery wall there is a job board that was not there yesterday. Nobody else looks at it. One posting is addressed to you.

```
  WORD FROM BELOW -- day 1:
  [q21] L8 Find the Evil Sword
  local hand: Sannl, grave robber
  pays 216g. take it within ~10
    days.
```

```
  options: the mine job, the board
    (4 more jobs here), the letter
    from below, the shops, travel
  Mindartis has 1 skill point
    unspent (`levelup`)
```

What do you do?
