## turn 2 (day 3)

> i travel to ensimaa and check quests there

Two days on the road and nothing on it. Ensimaa arrives as trees before it arrives as a city: Taivelle stands in a stand of great living trees beside the Avelune River, timber halls and stone walks around an open market. You come in through the western gate with the rain going sideways. The storm has had the roads since yesterday. Half the market is packed away, the awnings are roped down, and the river is loud under the bridge.

"Observe the drainage," Meriele says, water running off her scalp. "They built the walks to shed it. My people think in centuries and still cannot be persuaded to roof a market."

There is no king here. The high council takes petitions at the Crown Hall, and the porch out of the wet is where the day's work gets handed out.

Uthemar, the circle elder, is young for the title and wants his problem settled quietly. A group of mages stole songs out of the circle that they had no right to, and they are still singing them. The mist standing over the western valley is theirs. He wants the ritual stopped and the singers dealt with.

Erevan, the council's wizard, has the worse problem and says so straight. One of their own has rebelled, taken a tower on the burned road, and is setting fires from it. He wants the man killed. He is paying three hundred and forty-six gold because nobody in Taivelle will go.

The rest of the porch is out of your weight entirely: a tribute owed to a dragon, and two warden jobs the judges have posted, one of them paying in steel.
