## turn 6 (day 4)

> learn healing and alchemy. look for quests

You spend the evening on the two things you have been putting off: the mending cantrip drilled up past first aid, and a small copper still bought off a salt trader who wanted rid of it.

```
Martel deepens their healing: rank 2 --
  mend 5 HP (3 Power)
Martel studies the still: alchemy rank 1
  (batch 1, brewed stock cap 3) --
  unlocks healing potion, stamina
  draught
```

The Salt Road Inn takes your coin without comment and answers questions freely enough. By the second cup three people have been pointed at you and have come over.

Emen keeps the mountain pass and has stopped pretending the carts get through. Giants in the rock above the road, a cave they work out of, and she wants the cave emptied. Zug the warchief wants a rival band off his grazing and is blunt that it is a grudge, not a service. Snagg has the same complaint Sharn had, one valley over, and half the pay.

```
  Ordubal (day 4):
  [q53] L7  Giants in the Pass --
    3 fights, 277g. due day 8.
    (Emen, the pass keeper)
  [q54] L10 Rival Warband --
    1 fight, 180g. due day 8.
    (Zug, the warchief)
  [q55] L9  Dragon on the Mountain --
    1 fight, 162g. due day 6.
    (Snagg, the clan elder)
```

There is also a boy with a hunt job worth twenty-nine gold, who is told no by the innkeeper before he reaches you.

Word off the salt road runs further than that. Temenur has giants of its own and is paying properly for them, and Ulus-Gal has been trying to hire somebody for a wizard problem all week.

```
  around Tergal (1 day on the road):
  [q50] L13 Giants in the Pass --
    Temenur (2 days left)
  [q46] L10 The Renegade Wizard --
    Ulus-Gal (4 days left)
  [q45] L9  The Dragon's Tribute --
    Ulus-Gal (4 days left)
```

Late, the fire in the taproom hearth reads for one person again. Hell has sent a list of ideas.

```
  A SUGGESTION FROM BELOW: burglary,
    the land grab, grave-robbing, the
    protection racket, the tax cart,
    the village raid, the powder trade,
    the vault heist. (`crimes`)
```

Gwen reads it over your shoulder and says nothing at all.

```
  options: take a job here (q53, q54,
    q55), travel Temenur (q50, L13),
    the crime sheet (crimes), the
    tavern, camp
```
