## turn 1 (day 1)

> start a new game

You are Edwin: a Phyrascian, 27, with gold in your eyes. Strangers look at your eyes first. You throw ice, and you carry a zweihander. You have done this work for years. Years ago you also signed a contract with Hell -- wealth and power, obedience owed. The wealth has not arrived yet.

Osric walks beside you: 47, weathered, a gloomy man with a fine singing voice. He carries a wooden staff and knows the healing spell. He has watched your back for years.

You are in Harrowfield, a fishing village of Phyrascia, on R07C06. The houses stand above a shingle beach, and the boats are drawn up past the tide line. The sky is clear. The rains spoiled last year's harvest here, and the granary is half empty.

Godric, the sheriff, stops you at the door of the Heron Inn. He is 22 and his badge is new. "Bandits are robbing travelers on the king's road. Two days ago they took a wool cart and beat the driver. They keep a camp beside the road, not far from here. I have four men and none of them can fight. Find the camp and kill them, and the county pays you."

```
  job: Bandits on the Road -- L3
  giver: Godric, the sheriff
  1 site, 3 fights
  pay: 119s, 387 XP. due day 10.
```

Inside, the smoke from the taproom fire curls into words meant for you alone. Hell has work for you. Its local hand is Rowena, a fixer.

```
  WORD FROM BELOW: Desecrate the
    Shrine -- L4, dark
  hand: Rowena, the fixer
  pay: 172s, 352 XP (sin)
  take it by day 11.
```

```
  options: take the bandit job, the
    board (1 more here, 2 at
    Ivybourne), Rowena, spend your
    skill point, hunt, travel
```

What do you do?
