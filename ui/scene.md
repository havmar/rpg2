## turn 2 (day 2)

> take the dragon job

Sharn crosses your name onto the tally, in the space where the dead animals were, and sends a herd-boy ahead to say you are coming. The salt road bends north out of the basin and climbs. You camp at the foot of the passes; nothing comes.

Borugal Hills are bare stone cut with narrow passes. Above the last of the grazing there are tracks in the scree: three toes, deep, with a drag mark behind them. Whatever comes down off this mountain is not a dragon.

```

the mountain slopes (L13),
room 1/1: tracks
Wyvern 1 -- venomous sting
DEX 8  STR 7  STA 10  HP 26/26
breaks when beaten, a lashing tail
```

[the full fight, round by round](https://github.com/havmar/rpg2/blob/claude/new-game-7u0gyb/ui/fight-short.txt)

It comes off the high rock in one drop and lands between you. Your first bolt goes wide over its shoulder; the second opens it along the wing. Gwen works inside the tail and puts the katana in twice while the thing is still turning for her. It breaks for the ridge with the sting dragging and dies four strides up the scree. Neither of you is touched.

The herders come up the pass before evening, and Sharn with them. She looks at the sting a long while and then pays what a dragon costs, counted out on a flat rock. The goats are already moving back up the slope behind her.

Gwen cleans her blade twice and says she would rather not learn what else lives up here.
