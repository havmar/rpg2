## turn 10 (day 7)

> ownka drinks a potion, fight on

Ownka drinks and he comes off her and onto you, and puts his sabre through your side before Grushka can get a bolt out. You drink the last vial. Then Ownka feints, his guard opens, and she and Grushka land together -- six and six, in the same round. He is dead before he finishes falling.
