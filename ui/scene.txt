=== turn 1 (day 1) ===
> new game

You are Ivellios: an elf, twenty-five, thin, and dressed well above your means. A whip is coiled at your belt and a herbalist's kit rides in your coat. Some years ago you signed a contract with Hell. Wealth and power were promised. Nothing has arrived yet.

Dain walks beside you: a dwarf, thirty-four, a long-winded man who says everything in a whisper. He has watched your back for years. A duelist named Ilde is somewhere out in the world with his name on her list.

You are in Tomburgh, the walled capital of Firascir. The Flumenpur runs through the middle of it and the great east road crosses on a stone bridge. Carts are backed up at the east gate. Under the bridge arch a man beats wool in the shallows.

Doran stops you on the bridge road. He is the head shepherd, his coat is out at both shoulders, and he has a fine voice for bad news. Wolves came down into the high pasture. They killed sheep, then they killed a shepherd. He will not go back up there himself and he says so without shame.

  job: Wolves Attack -- L1
  giver: Doran, head shepherd
  2 sites, 2 fights. pay: 29g
  due day 7

He is not the only one asking. The priest at the village graveyard has been standing in the road all morning telling people the dead are getting out.

  job: The Restless Crypt -- L1
  giver: Brand II, village priest
  1 site, 3 fights. pay: 40g
  due day 6

By the tannery wall there is a job board that was not there yesterday. Nobody else looks at it. One notice is addressed to you.

  WORD FROM BELOW: Sacrifice the Puppy
    at Tomburgh -- take it by day 11.
    the local hand: Aldric, a fixer

  options: the wolves, the crypt,
    the notice from below, the board
    (3 harder jobs), hunt, travel

What do you do?
