## turn 1 (day 1)

> please start a new game

You are Pox: a goblin, 33, with fire in your hands and a bright staff to hold it steady. A long career of paid work behind you, a purse of 1763 gold, and a contract with Hell signed so long ago that the terms have gone quiet. The next letter is pinned to your next level.

Myev has walked at your side for years: an orc, 29, tattooed to the jaw, praying in a whisper, carrying a zweihander she has drilled with since before you met her. She has never asked what the contract said.

You are in Castavera, walled capital of Mortellaria, white stone and tiled roofs above the Stura River road. Haze over the market, and it has not lifted since dawn.

Sabine the sheriff finds you at the fountain steps -- human, forty-odd, mud to the knee. The east road has been shut a week. Not boys with knives: deserters who know their trade, camped where the road bends, and she has buried two of her own men trying. She wants the camp cleared out and she wants it inside the week.

```
  job: Bandits on the Road -- L17
  giver: Sabine, the sheriff
  1 site, 3 encounters
  pay: 673g, 1742 XP
  5 days left
```

A priest off the Ensimaa border has a crowd at the far side of the market. The graves up there are empty, he says, and the dead are walking. A human king who serves a dark god is marching an army that raises itself out of the ground, and the reason given is a pretender the Ensimaa court is sheltering. Uthemar, speaker of the high council, is calling for blades at Taivelle.

```
  WORD OF THE WAR (day 1)
  [w1] L2 The Dead Rise -- 2 sites,
    taken at Taivelle (Ensimaa)
```
