## turn 1 (day 1)

> new game please

You are Theren: an elf, forty years old, a magic user. You throw fire. You can also blink a short distance, which puts you behind a man before he has turned around. Years ago you signed a contract with Hell -- wealth and power promised, nothing asked of you yet. You carry a masterwork rapier and a purse of 173 gold. You have been doing this work a long time.

Snizzle walks with you: a goblin, thirty-two, a soldier before he was anything else. He is loud, he looks unwell and always has, and he has watched your back for years. He fights with a wooden staff and knows enough healing to keep you standing.

You are in Maketawa, the goblin capital of Gibili. The city crowds both banks of the Wela River -- brick halls, metal roofs, market awnings. The city is rich and busy. The sky is clear.

A herald stands at the Rivet Gate, announcing the war. An orc warlord has united the clans and means to take every land his army can reach. The reason given is that the Gibili lands have not submitted to the Sky. Orc raiders are already burning guard posts on the border and mapping the roads. Dizzy, the chief overboss, is calling for blades.

```
  *** WORD OF THE WAR (day 1) ***
  [w1] L2 Orc Scouts -- 2 sites
  giver: Dizzy, chief overboss
  pay: 79g, 290 XP. no deadline.
```

Wozzek is at the gatehouse with a different problem. He commands the border, he is twenty-one, and he has lost three forts in a month -- not to the orcs. A giant came down out of the hills, walked through the wall of the first fort and then through the next two, and went back up to its hall. He wants it tracked there and killed, and he wants the head brought back so he can show people it is dead. He wants that inside four days.

```
  job: The Giant at the Border -- L6
  giver: Wozzek, border commander
  pay: 199g, 493 XP. due day 5.
  2 sites, 2 fights.
  proof: the giant's head
```

```
  also asking at Maketawa:
  [q22] L6 The Boiler Cult -- Mizzle,
    the factory inspector; pays a
    masterwork rapier
  [q24] L6 Stolen Workers -- Zeena,
    the night shift boss; 124g
  (4 more jobs on the board)
```

```
  options: take the giant job, the
    war call, hear the rest of the
    board, the market, spend
    Theren's 1 skill point, hunt,
    travel
```

What do you do?
