## turn 1 (day 1)

> please start a new game

You are Bogdan, a Vellisclav boy of twelve. You throw fire, and ice when fire will not do. You carry a master smith's staff, and the purse on your belt holds 242 silver from years of this work. Some years ago you signed a contract with Hell: wealth and power for you, obedience for them. Hell sends work now and then.

Danilo walks beside you. He is 27, born poor in this same country, and he dresses better than anyone in the village. He sings on the road. He carries a plain staff and can close a wound with his hands. He has watched your back for years.

You are in Uzhany, a fishing village of Vellisclavia, on R03C28 -- timber country on the northern sea. The boats are drawn up on the shingle above the tide line. There was a hard frost in the night, and the nets on the racks are stiff with it.

Ludmila, the village priest, stops you on the path up from the beach. "The dead are walking out of our graveyard," she says. "There is a crypt under the wayside shrine at the end of the burial ground. They come up out of it at night. Go down and destroy them." She has no silver to pay with. The church owns one thing of value, a staff from a master smith's forge, as good as yours, and she offers that.

```
  job: The Restless Crypt -- L7
  giver: Ludmila, village priest
  pay: a masterwork wooden staff,
    352 XP. due day 6.
```

Earlier this morning, in the taproom of the Heron Inn, the smoke over the fire turned into words that only you could read. Cultists near Uzhany are calling up a demon, and heroes are coming to stop them. Hell wants you to hold the cultists' camp until the demon is through. Hell's man in Uzhany is a masked collector named Mstislav.

```
  WORD FROM BELOW: Guard the Cultists
    -- L7. pay: 189s, 352 XP (sin).
    hand: Mstislav, the masked
      collector. take it by day 11.
```

```
  options: take the crypt job, the
    board (1 more job), Hell's job,
    hunt, travel
```

What do you do?
