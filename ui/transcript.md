## turn 1 (day 1)

> please start a new game

You are Martel: human, 38, a fire-caster with thirteen years of paid work behind you. The staff in your hand is a focused one, banded and burn-marked at the head. Somewhere back in that career you signed a contract with Hell -- wealth and power, terms pending. The terms are still pending.

Gwen has watched your back for years. Human, thin and pale, dressed better than the road deserves, a katana she has drilled with for years. She would rather not fight. She is very good at it.

You are in Ordubal, a low-walled town on the salt road in Tergal. The great cistern holds the basin's only good water, and the yard around it is packed with salt carts and penned goats. Orc drovers argue prices over the animals' heads.

Sharn finds you at the pens: clan elder, young for the title, a herd tally in her fist with a third of it crossed out. Something comes off the high peaks and takes her animals off the slopes in daylight. She calls it a dragon. She wants somebody to climb up to the nest and kill it there, and she wants it done inside three days, before the herds move down.

```
  job: Dragon on the Mountain -- L13
  giver: Sharn, the clan elder
  pay: 234g. due day 4 (3 days left).
```

A rider comes through the gate while she is still talking, dwarf, road-filthy, shouting for the caravan masters. The goblin bosses have made one government of themselves. They are taking animals, tools and people off the Dvarvengrond border, and the high thane is calling blades north.

```
  WORD OF THE WAR: the Thousand
    Workshops. Riswynn, high thane,
    calls blades to Bjorgheim --
    [w1] L2 Stolen Parts.
```

In the taproom of the Salt Road Inn the hearth smoke holds its shape long enough to be read. Nobody else looks up.

```
  WORD FROM BELOW: [q42] L13 DARK
    Open the Hellgate -- pays 772g.
    take it by day 11.
    local hand: Myev, the fixer.
```

```
  Martel: 1 skill point unspent
  options: the dragon job, the board
    (3 more jobs), the hellgate, hunt,
    travel
```

What do you do?

## turn 2 (day 2)

> take the dragon job

Sharn crosses your name onto the tally, in the space where the dead animals were, and sends a herd-boy ahead to say you are coming. The salt road bends north out of the basin and climbs. You camp at the foot of the passes; nothing comes.

Borugal Hills are bare stone cut with narrow passes. Above the last of the grazing there are tracks in the scree: three toes, deep, with a drag mark behind them. Whatever comes down off this mountain is not a dragon.

```
=== Dragon on the Mountain ===
the mountain slopes (L13),
room 1/1: tracks
Wyvern 1 -- venomous sting
DEX 8  STR 7  STA 10  HP 26/26
breaks when beaten, a lashing tail
```

[the full fight, round by round](https://github.com/havmar/rpg2/blob/claude/new-game-7u0gyb/ui/fight-short.txt)

It comes off the high rock in one drop and lands between you. Your first bolt goes wide over its shoulder; the second opens it along the wing. Gwen works inside the tail and puts the katana in twice while the thing is still turning for her. It breaks for the ridge with the sting dragging and dies four strides up the scree. Neither of you is touched.

The herders come up the pass before evening, and Sharn with them. She looks at the sting a long while and then pays what a dragon costs, counted out on a flat rock. The goats are already moving back up the slope behind her.

Gwen cleans her blade twice and says she would rather not learn what else lives up here.
