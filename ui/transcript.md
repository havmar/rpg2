## turn 1 (day 1)

> please start a new game

You are Nerva, thirty-two, a Byzantine, and you have been taking paid work for years. You throw ice. The staff you carry is the best thing you own and it cost more than everything else in your kit together. Your purse holds 115 silver. Years ago you signed a contract with Hell -- wealth and power promised, obedience owed. Hell has been collecting the obedience ever since.

Varro walks beside you. He is seventeen, he looks older than that, and he has worked at your side since he was a boy. He carries a zweihander taller than he is, keeps his coat brushed, and stops for every dog in every village you pass through.

You are in Salicetum, a fishing village on the Byzantine coast, where the road comes down off the hills to a sheltered cove. Boats are drawn up on the shingle. The hills behind the village are terraced in vines, and the vines failed last year in the drought. Venice is an hour up the same road; the spice road out of Cairo ends at its docks.

Aurelia, the village priest, meets you at the graveyard wall. She is thirty-four and she has not slept. The dead walk out of this graveyard at night, she says. Two nights running, and last night one of them got as far as the fish sheds. The crypt under the yard is the village ossuary, the walls stacked with the village's own bones going back four hundred years, and she says the bones are not stacked any more. She wants you to go down and destroy what is in there. She can pay 166 silver and she wants it done inside the week.

```
  job: The Restless Crypt -- L5
  giver: Aurelia, village priest
  1 site, 2 fights. pays 166s.
  due day 7 -- 6 days left
```

A rider came up the coast road yesterday with the emperor's call-up. Umaia's army is over the border and Byzantium is taking every man in the east. The fighting is a long way from this cove.

There is a job board on the fish shed wall this morning. The wall was bare yesterday, and nobody in the village can see it. The work on it is yours: an altar in a chapel, blood on it before dawn, and the board says any blood will do, including your own. A fixer named Paulina holds the job here, and she lives in the village.

## turn 2 (day 1)

> doing the crypt job

Aurelia takes you up the coast road to Venice. Salicetum buries its dead outside the city wall, along with the poor of Petrapolis and Castranova -- one walled field for all three, and a hooded brotherhood to do the digging. It is an hour's walk uphill and the market noise comes over the wall most of the way.

The graveyard is flat stones in rough grass, with the city stacked up the slope behind it. A stone stair goes down into the ossuary at the far end of the field. Two stones near the wall have been pushed off and left lying. Four of the dead are standing among the graves, and they do not move when you come through the gate.

Varro's zweihander is into the first skeleton before the lines meet. Your ice does most of the rest: three of the four go down to it, and Varro takes the second skeleton apart in the same round it swings at him. Neither of you is touched. By the last ghoul you are Winded and down to one Power.

Aurelia waits at the gate and does not come in. Varro cleans his blade in the grass and looks at the stair. "After you," he says.
