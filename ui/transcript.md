## turn 1 (day 1)

> please start a new game

You are Gruk, out of Tergal, thirty-two years old. You throw ice. You have been throwing it since you were a boy and nobody on the steppe finds that strange. You carry a magic staff, and the cuts it leaves do not close. Years of paid fighting bought the staff and the thousand silver in your purse. Somewhere in those years you signed a contract with Hell. Wealth and power were promised and obedience was owed. Nobody down there has asked you for anything in a long while.

Gell walks with you: a Tergal man of thirty-five, armored, a zweihander across his back. He is religious and he has a singing voice. He has watched your back for years.

You are in Sargul, a herding village on the open grass. Corrals on all four sides, low houses, one well. The place lives on horses. Kyiv is a day east.

Varg meets you at the corral fence. He is the border captain here, and he is twenty-three. Another warband has been working the herd trails north of the village. They have taken horses twice this month and killed two boys who were watching them. He wants their camp found and the men in it dead. He has no silver for it. He has a rapier that came off a dead man three years ago, and he will hand it over when the work is done.

```
  job: Rival Warband -- L14
  giver: Varg, the border captain
  pays: a true rapier (+2 attack)
  1 site, 2 fights. due day 6.
```

## turn 2 (day 1)

> take the warband job

Varg shakes on it and points you north. The trail is an hour out of the village: churned grass, a dead horse with its haunches cut away, and the watch posted where the ground rises. Three men. They see you coming and do not run.

```
=== Rival Warband ===
the raided trail (L14),
room 1/2: outer watch
Warband Veteran 1 -- longsword
DEX 6  STR 5  STA 7  HP 13/13
takes spoils, leaves the fallen
2x War Chief -- longsword
DEX 7  STR 6  STA 8  HP 17/17
drilled +2,
takes spoils, leaves the fallen
```

[the full fight, round by round](https://github.com/havmar/rpg2/blob/claude/new-game-rhc7vd/ui/fight-short.txt)

Your ice takes the veteran twice and holds him frozen both times, and he never swings once. Gell's sweep catches him and a chief together and kills him outright. Then the second chief gets inside Gell's guard three rounds running: ribs, back, belly. Gell is still on his feet with his zweihander up. Your Power is gone.
