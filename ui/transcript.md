## turn 1 (day 1)

> start a new game

You are Imsh: an orc, 29, with ice in your hands that nobody taught you and a cruel katana on your hip. Eleven years of paid work stand behind you and 644 gold rides in the purse. The contract with Hell is older than the work. Wealth and power were promised. So far Hell has mostly sent letters.

Urzoth walks beside you: orc, a craftsman before she was anything else, a zweihander across her back and better clothes than the work calls for. She mumbles when she talks. She has taken blows meant for you for years.

You are in Dunmaelle, a timber town of Ensimaa. The gate stands between two old beeches. The western forest road runs out through it.

Keyleth catches you in the town hall -- an elf, the council judge, a stack of complaints weighted down with a stone. Wardens hold the forest road. They have decided the road is theirs and travelers pay to use it. Two carters paid this week; the third turned back. She wants them off it.

```
  job: Rogue Wardens -- L11
  giver: Keyleth, council judge
  pay: 317g, 845 XP. due day 6
    (turned in now it pays quick)
```

A rider came through the gate ahead of you. Goblins over the Firascir border, taking animals, tools and people. King Hale is calling blades to Tomburgh.

```
  WORD OF THE WAR: the Thousand
    Workshops -- the goblin bosses
    have united.
  [w1] L2 Stolen Parts, at Tomburgh
```

In the lane behind the hall a job board stands where a bare wall stood yesterday. Nobody else in the lane looks at it.

```
  WORD FROM BELOW: Guard the
    Cultists (L12), here in
    Dunmaelle -- 324g. take it by
    day 10.
  local hand: Bethrynna, the
    masked collector
```

```
  options: take the wardens job,
    ask around (3 more jobs here),
    the board from below, the inn,
    hunt, travel
```

What do you do?

## turn 2 (day 1)

> do the wardens

Keyleth writes the warrant out and stamps it herself. An hour west the beeches close over the road and the toll post is in sight: a felled trunk across the ruts, three wardens behind it in green, and a hand held up for you to stop.

```
=== Rogue Wardens ===
the forest road (L11),
room 1/2: road approach
Warden Captain 1 -- longsword
DEX 6  STR 5  STA 7  HP 13/13
takes spoils, leaves the fallen
2x Forest Champion -- longsword
DEX 7  STR 6  STA 8  HP 17/17
drilled +2,
takes spoils, leaves the fallen
```

[the full fight, round by round](https://github.com/havmar/rpg2/blob/claude/new-game-0jbyo0/ui/fight-short.txt)

You take one champion's mind before anyone draws, and he puts his longsword through his own captain. The captain goes down under your ice a round later, the puppet after him. That leaves the third, and the third has been cutting Urzoth apart the whole time: a gash across the back, a cracked skull, a torn side. She has the zweihander up and is not giving ground.

## turn 3 (day 1)

> resume --heal Urzoth

Urzoth gets the vial down one-handed and keeps the zweihander between her and the last warden. It turns into a long grind after that: your bolts deflect, one misfires in your own hand, and the champion cuts you both up small while nobody can finish anybody. He runs when his breath goes, bleeding, and neither of you has the legs to chase.

Two longswords lie in the ruts, worth about what the toll took. The katana is warm in your hand; it always is after it kills.

The crossing is another half hour up the road, and it is the last of the job. Urzoth mumbles something about the light going. Your Power is at 0/10.

```
  options: press on to the crossing,
    back to Dunmaelle (a bed knits
    a wound a night; healer, shop),
    camp here on the road
```
