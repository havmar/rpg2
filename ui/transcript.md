## turn 1 (day 1)

> start a new game

You are Vanuath: an elf, 32, thin, loud, and hungry most of the time. One rank of ice magic and a wooden staff are the whole of your trade. Years ago you signed with Hell. The wealth and the power are still pending. The obedience is not.

Gruk walks beside you: an orc, 33, friendly and a great talker, though he says everything in a whisper. A sling at his belt, fire at his fingers. He has watched your back for years.

You are in Valesero, a farming village in Mortellaria. An irrigation channel runs the length of the low wall, reeds down to the water. Carts stand in the square with nobody unloading them.

Jorik stops you at the bridge -- army captain, forty-odd, mumbling into his collar and swearing between the words. Soldiers he used to command have gone over the wire. They burned a farm out past the reeds and they are camped somewhere behind it. He wants them found and finished, and he wants it this week.

```
  job: Deserter Raiders -- L1
  giver: Jorik, army captain
  1 site, 1 fight. pay: 18g
  due day 5 -- turned in now, x1.15
```

That evening the smoke over your table in the inn curls into words. Nobody else in the room looks up.

```
  WORD FROM BELOW: Guard the
    Cultists -- L2, at Valesero.
    pays 54g. take it by day 11.
    the local hand: Garrick.
```

```
  options: the deserters job, the
    letter from below, the board
    (1 more job -- L6), hunt,
    travel
```

What do you do?
