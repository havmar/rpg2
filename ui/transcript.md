## turn 1 (day 1)

> new game

You are Caladrel: an elf, 38. You speak in a whisper, and at length, and you are not kind. Fire answers when you call it. Years ago you signed a contract with Hell -- wealth and power, terms pending. So far the terms are still pending.

Corvin walks with you: human, twenty-seven, neat coat, formal about everything, a coiled whip at his belt and his mind mostly on his next meal. He has watched your back for years.

You are in Portomera, a harbor town of Mortellaria. Stone quays hold back a deep cove; fishing boats unload onto the market flags. The air is tar and gutted fish.

Hale finds you before you find him. An army captain in dented plate, loud enough to turn the fish sellers around. Deserters -- his own men a season ago -- have been burning farms up the coast. He knows the burned farm they camp at. He wants them stopped, and he wants it done before the week turns.

```
  job: Deserter Raiders -- L1
  giver: Hale, army captain (loud)
  pay: 18g. due day 4.
```

At the inn door the taproom smoke bends into words as you pass, and holds them long enough to read. Hell wants a rare beast taken alive. A fixer named Berrick holds the job in town.

```
  WORD FROM BELOW: Capture the
    Beast at Portomera -- take it
    by day 11.
```

```
  options: take the deserter job,
    the board (3 more jobs),
    Berrick's job, hunt, travel
```

What do you do?

## turn 2 (day 2)

> take the deserter job

Hale shakes on it and tells you where they are: a day up the coast road, above Doramonte.

You camp on the road. Doramonte is a fishing village in a cove, nets on the rails, the smell of the sheds coming up the hill. The farm above it is a black frame with no roof left. Two men are sitting in the yard with their backs to the house, and they stand when they see you.

```
=== Deserter Raiders ===
the burned farm (L1),
room 1/1: outer watch
Bruiser 1 -- spear
DEX 4  STR 5  STA 5  HP 9/9
takes spoils, leaves the fallen
Bruiser 2 -- dagger
DEX 4  STR 5  STA 5  HP 9/9
takes spoils, leaves the fallen
```

[the full fight, round by round](https://github.com/havmar/rpg2/blob/claude/new-game-rdcda7/ui/fight-short.txt)

It is fire from the first exchange. The spearman takes your bolt coming in and Corvin's on top of it, and never reaches steel range whole. The second dies the same way a round later and swings at Corvin's head on the way down; Corvin turns it with the whip. Neither of them touches you.

```
The party catches its breath (+1 STA)
Caladrel +35 XP (encounter) [35/100]
Corvin +35 XP (encounter) [35/100]
Left among the dead:
a spear (+0 atk/+0 sev, 5g),
a dagger (+0 atk/-1 sev, 2g).

*** QUEST COMPLETE: Deserter Raiders --
the burned farm. Reward: 18 gold. ***
Caladrel talks the pay up:
+2g (20g in all).
The party purse holds 20 gold.
Caladrel +53 XP (quest) [88/100]
Corvin +53 XP (quest) [88/100]
Corvin: satisfaction 7 -> 8/10
(a job paid out)
EPILOGUE (day 2): The deserters are
  defeated. Their weapons return to the
  army.

-- the party --
Caladrel: Unhurt STA 6/8 Power 1/4
  (stamina x1)
Corvin: Unhurt STA 5/7 Power 2/5
  (healing x1, stamina x2)
Purse 20g; day 2.
```

Hale comes up the coast road with a wagon before noon. He counts the coin out on the wagon board, loud enough for the whole village, and loads the dead men's weapons himself. He knew both of them by name and says both names while he works.

The spear is plain army issue and still cuts better than the hammer you carry, if you want it before it goes on the cart.

```
  options: the spear, the local
    board (2 jobs, L6 and L7),
    back to Portomera for
    Berrick's job, hunt, camp
```
